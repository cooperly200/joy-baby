Help Menu

What is this Game?

Joy-Baby is a game that hopes to be the answer to the following burning questions:

  • How can I help my baby develop their capability to interact with objects?
  • How can I teach my baby they can control object remotely?
  • What game can I show my baby that they think is so cool they start grinning wildly as they play it?

How to Produce Nice Sounds

  • Try begining by moving the controller up
  • Those slices through the circle mark the zones of chords in a song
  • Proceed from zone to zone in a clockwise manor at a pace that feels nice to you
  • The Faster you move the joystick, the faster the the music will place
  • As the joystick gets further from the center, the quality of the sound will change
  • Pressing a button on the gamepad will cycle to the next part of the song

Things to Try with your Baby

  • Set the baby up securely in a bouncer
  • Don't play for too long; if they seem fussy, then they're ready for a nap
  • Hold the full sized joystick (recommended) or game pad near the baby's hands
  • Introduce the baby slowly, at first maybe only you push the joystick and look at the response from the screen
  • It's ok if they can only push the stick in one direction, try rotating the joystick for them. I might have a chance to add more modes to the game that make the game less instrument-like and easier for it to produce pleasing tones automatically
